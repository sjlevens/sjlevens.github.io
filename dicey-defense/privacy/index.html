Privacy policy

This policy explains how Dicey Defense handles game saves, purchases, advertising, privacy choices and support information.

Effective date: 2 November 2026 (previous version: 8 October 2026)

Who is responsible

Dicey Defense is provided by Samuel Levens ("we", "us").

Privacy and support contact: sjlevens+dicey@gmail.com.

Game saves on your device

The game saves your unlocks, in-game currency, settings, records, challenge results, achievements, chosen dice set, purchase ownership and current run on your device. A local backup helps recover a damaged save. These records let you resume play and preserve your progress.

The current game does not create a player account or send your save to a developer-operated server. There is no game-operated cloud sync. Device backups and restoration provided by Apple, Google or your device manufacturer are separate services governed by their settings and policies.

Purchases and restoring ownership

The game offers optional one-time purchases through the App Store and Google Play: the Supporter Pack (no ads, +50% kill income banked after each run, an automatic +50% payout bonus, the Patron dice set and a Supporter badge), cosmetic dice sets sold individually or as a bundle, and two content expansions. Relic Set VII adds relic choices to free runs; Act 4 adds waves 31–40 and can be switched off for future free runs. Daily and weekly challenges always use the base game. There are no subscriptions.

Apple and Google process payment under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment-card details. The game receives purchase information such as the product, transaction identifier, purchase token or signed transaction so it can verify the purchase, save the unlocked benefit, confirm delivery and restore ownership. A store ownership check can also update a benefit after a refund or another change the store reports. Supporter kill income accrues only while ownership is active; a refund stops future accrual without removing income already earned.

Ownership is saved on your device. Restoring purchases contacts the store and uses the store account that owns them. Clearing the game's data removes progress but does not cancel a store purchase. A run already in progress keeps the expansion content it started with, even if a later ownership check removes that content from future runs. There is no developer-operated purchase-verification server or game account.

On iPhone and iPad, the game uses Apple's StoreKit directly. On Android, it uses Unity In-App Purchasing to connect to Google Play. That SDK also reports purchase-related information to Unity, including transaction information, app and installation identifiers, device and session information and consent state. The game sets Unity's optional analytics and advertising consent to denied; this does not stop the SDK's purchase reporting. See Unity's IAP privacy disclosure and privacy policy.

For purchase or privacy help, contact the address above. Where Unity processes purchase information on our behalf, we can help route a verified access or deletion request to Unity; where Unity acts independently, its disclosure explains how to contact it directly.

Optional ads and Google services

Dicey Defense uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. You can play without choosing a rewarded ad.

Google's services can process the following information, depending on your platform, choices and applicable requirements:

  • IP address, including an estimate of your general location.
  • Advertising, device, app or account-related identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Ad views, clicks, video interactions and other app-interaction information.
  • Crash, diagnostic and performance information.
  • Privacy choices and information needed to determine which consent messages apply.

This information supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, service performance, privacy compliance and fraud prevention. Ad personalization depends on your choices and eligibility. Google and the advertising partners involved in serving an ad may process information for these purposes; applicable consent messages identify providers and choices.

We do not collect gameplay analytics ourselves. Google's advertising services process the interaction and diagnostic information described above, and on Android the purchase SDK reports purchase-related information to Unity, as described under Purchases.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services, and Google's SDK disclosures for Android and iOS.

Your advertising choices

The app checks advertising privacy requirements when it starts. Where required, a consent message appears before an ad can be requested. Ads are requested only when Google's consent status permits them.

When available, use Ad privacy options in the collection or Ad privacy during a run to review or change your choices. Your device also provides advertising and tracking controls. Declining personalization does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your age group (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) before your first optional ad and saves the answer on your device. Players under 13 are not shown ads, players aged 13–17 receive restricted ad treatment, and ads are not personalized. Only the resulting ad treatment is sent to Google with ad requests.

Privacy checks are separate from watching an ad and may contact Google even if you do not choose an ad.

If you contact us

If you send a support or privacy request, we receive the contact information and message you provide, plus any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to include. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or information unrelated to your issue.

Our contact address uses Gmail, so Google also processes support email under its Privacy Policy.

We keep support and privacy correspondence only for as long as needed to handle requests, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. We review it periodically and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain in the app's storage until that storage is removed. On Android, clear the game's app storage in system settings. On iOS, delete the app rather than offloading it. These actions erase local progress; check your device's backup settings separately if you also want to remove backup copies. We cannot remotely erase or restore a save that exists only on your device.

There is no game account to delete. For information you have sent directly to us, contact the privacy address above. Apple, Google, Unity and other service providers control retention and deletion within their services under their own policies and applicable law. Deleting the game does not itself request deletion of information already processed by those providers, including store transaction records. See Google's retention information.

Security and processing locations

Local saves use your device's app storage and its access protections. Google's advertising service encrypts information in transit. Apple, Google, Unity and the website hosting provider may process information outside your country under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

Audience and children's privacy

Dicey Defense is intended for teens and adults and is not directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and remove information where appropriate.

This website
