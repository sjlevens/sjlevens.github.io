03 · About

Interfaces can be awesome, or they can suck.

I started coding games on a TI-84 Plus in maths class and passing them around by link cable. That's when I learned the difference, and decided to build the awesome kind.

I like modular, readable, functional-lite code, systems that keep working when nobody's watching, and picturing a real person using whatever I'm making before I write a line.

Day to day I work AI-native: several Claude Code and Codex agents in parallel worktrees, written plans before code, and agent-run reviews before big merges. Design, review and the final call stay with me.

From 2023 to 2024 I volunteered as a career mentor with headspace, helping young people work out their next step.
